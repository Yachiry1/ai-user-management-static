Negroni

Ingredients

  • 30 ml gin
  • 30 ml Campari
  • 30 ml sweet vermouth
  • Orange peel

Instructions

Stir all ingredients with ice for 20 seconds, strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube, and garnish with orange peel.
